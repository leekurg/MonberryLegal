Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27.09.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ilya Anikin (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the Monberry mobile application (the “App”).

Short version: The App does not collect personal or non-personal data automatically. No user-identifying data is transmitted when the App makes network requests. The only information we may receive is information that a user voluntarily provides when contacting support.

1. Information We Receive

Information Provided to Support

If you contact us, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your name, email address, the content of your request, attachments, screenshots, or other information you voluntarily provide.

We use this information only to review and respond to your request, provide support, and address technical issues.

No Automatic Data Collection

The App does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or other software development kits that automatically collect information about users.

We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, precise or approximate location, usage events, diagnostics, or user activity data through the App.

Network Requests

The App may make network requests to provide its functionality. These requests do not transmit personal data or other user data that would allow us to identify a user.

We do not intentionally include names, email addresses, account identifiers, financial records, or other user-identifying information in such requests.

Network providers may process technical information required to deliver network traffic, such as an IP address, under their own policies. The App does not use that information to identify users or send it to us for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

2. How We Use Support Information

Information voluntarily provided in a support request may be used to communicate with you, investigate and resolve the issue, maintain the App, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use support information for advertising or behavioral analytics.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share user information for advertising or analytics.

We may disclose information from a support request only when reasonably necessary to respond to the request, comply with a legal obligation, protect rights and safety, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

4. Storage and Deletion

Support messages may remain in our email or support systems for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain support records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you provided by contacting us at the address below. We will consider and handle the request as required by applicable law.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal data to us through support, please contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

6. Changes to This Policy

From time to time, We may make changes to this Policy at Our discretion. Users can learn about such changes from the last-updated date at the beginning of the Policy or from a notice in the App.

If the User does not agree with the current Policy, in whole or in part, or does not agree with any changes, the User should stop using the Service and delete the App.

The User should refrain from using the Service in the future until the terms of this Policy and the User Agreement are acceptable to the User.

7. Contact Information

For any questions regarding the processing of personal data of Monberry App Users, please contact Us for clarification.

Send your email to monberry@proton.me and use the subject line: “Question about personal data processing” .

We will review your question and make reasonable efforts to respond as soon as possible.
