Last updated: 15.08.2026

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter — Policy) explains the procedure for processing data of Users of the Monberry Service (hereinafter — Service) by Ilya Anikin (hereinafter — Operator, We, Us, Our, etc.).

A brief summary of the Policy

  1. We collect Users' data. This data can be personal or non-personal.
  2. All User data is collected directly from Users, for example when a User contacts Our support center, or collected automatically, including User device data, identifiers, and data about User actions in the Service.
  3. All data is collected on a legal basis. We may rely on contractual obligations, legitimate interests, or User consent. Such consent can be withdrawn at any time.
  4. Collected data is used to maintain the Service. In some cases, We may share data with Third parties for analytics, advertising, or other purposes described in this Policy.
  5. If We need to share Users' personal data with Third parties for new purposes that were not previously disclosed, We will obtain additional User consent where required by applicable law.
  6. All data is stored for the minimum period necessary to maintain the Service and fulfill the Operator's obligations.
  7. To obtain further details about the processing of User personal data, please review the full text of this Policy below. You can also contact Us at monberry@proton.me with the subject line “Question about personal data processing”.

The comprehensive Policy text is provided below.

1. Terms and definitions

Personal data — any data about a User that identifies or allows the User to be identified as a person, either directly or indirectly. When connected to an individual, information such as their first name, last name, contact number, and email address can be classified as personal data.

User — a person who has reached the necessary age for using the App legally, according to applicable age restrictions and laws.

Service — a combination of the App, databases, and other technical elements that provide Users with access to the functionality and content of Monberry.

App — the Monberry mobile application acquired from the official store, which provides Users with access to the Monberry Service.

Data processing — a series of actions, whether simple or complex, that encompass the collection, analysis, sharing, storage, anonymization, and deletion of data. In this Policy, We use the term “processing” for automatic processing only because the Operator does not process personal data in any other way.

Operator — a person or company that processes Users' personal data on a legal basis.

Anonymized data — information that has been irreversibly altered to remove any direct or indirect links to a specific individual. This results in a dataset associated with a particular User without making it possible to identify that User.

Sensitive data — a special category of data relating to information about the racial or ethnic origin of the User, political preferences, attitudes towards religion and other beliefs, membership in societies and trade unions, sexual orientation, biometric and genetic information, health, and other sensitive data.

Third parties — persons or companies that are not affiliated with or controlled by Us.

2. What personal data do we collect?

While providing Users with access to Our Service, We collect certain data about Our Users. This data may be used for User identification. Collected data is divided according to the way it is obtained.

Data shared by the User

While using the Service, a User may contact Us for various reasons, including contacting support, submitting a claim, or requesting clarification regarding terms and rules.

We may access all data provided by the User in such an inquiry. This data may include, but is not limited to, the User's name, email address, phone number, contact information, photographs, User ID, and the contents of the User's message.

Data collected automatically

When accessing the Service, the User's device may automatically transmit certain data to Us because this data may be necessary for Service maintenance, advertising system maintenance, and effectiveness analysis.

The list of automatically collected data may include, but is not limited to, the name and version of the operating system, IP address, device identifiers, bundle identifier, App version, device information, device type, screen resolution, model, brand, advertising identifiers, device language, date and time, approximate device geolocation, records and data about User activity in the App, User pseudo-identifier, and other technical data.

All assignable identifiers are stored on the User's device. If the User removes the App, the identifiers may be reset. If the User downloads the App again, new identifiers may be assigned.

3. How do We use User data?

Any processing and use of data is based on a legal basis. The legal basis for data processing may include the following:

Personal data processing for the performance of contractual obligations

Contractual obligations may serve as a legal basis for processing data for Service maintenance, technical support, App updates, consideration of complaints and inquiries, compliance with content policies and marketplace rules, selection of device-compatible functionality, localization settings, and other related purposes.

Processing necessary to fulfill legitimate interests

We may process the minimum amount of data necessary to fulfill legitimate interests that do not infringe upon the rights and freedoms of Users.

Such legitimate interests may include processing User data for security purposes, analyzing Service performance, studying User behavior, identifying opportunities to improve the Service, analyzing advertising effectiveness, collecting statistics, and conducting market research.

Processing based on User consent

Processing of personal data may be carried out on the basis of the User's voluntary and informed consent. This consent may be changed, withdrawn, or refused at any time.

The absence or withdrawal of consent will not necessarily prevent the User from accessing the Service. However, it may limit the User's ability to receive personalized advertising offers or personalized content selections based on User data.

Purposes for which User consent may be required may include, but are not limited to, providing personalized content, displaying personalized advertising, showing advertisements based on the User's approximate location or language, analyzing User interactions with advertisements, creating a personalized advertising profile, and analyzing User activity for advertising purposes.

In cases expressly provided for in this Policy, We may transfer the personal data of Users to Third parties. Such Third parties may include, but are not limited to:

  • Our affiliates;
  • Service providers performing certain activities on Our behalf;

We may also transfer non-personal User data, which does not contain information that can identify the User as a specific person, to third-party companies for advertising, analytics, and improving the quality of the Service.

In such transfers, We require third-party recipients to comply with reasonable security requirements. We do not intentionally share personal data received directly from Users together with non-personal technical data in a way that would identify the User as a specific individual, except where necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or required by law.

When processing a User's personal data, We comply with reasonable security requirements intended to prevent loss, leakage, theft, unauthorized access, and other unlawful actions involving data.

However, no system can provide a 100% guarantee of data security on the Internet. We also cannot guarantee that all Third parties receiving User data from Us or processing data under their own privacy policies will comply with every necessary security measure.

6. User data storage

When processing Users' personal data, We strive to keep it to the minimum amount and for the shortest period necessary.

We will delete User data when it is no longer needed for the purposes described in this Policy or after receiving a valid request from the User to delete personal data, unless further storage is required by law or is necessary to resolve a dispute or fulfill a legal obligation.

If a User chooses to use the Service again, previously deleted data may be replaced with new data collected in accordance with the current version of this Policy.

In certain cases, We may retain User personal data for a longer period than necessary to fulfill Our Service obligations, including in connection with User communications, complaints, legal disputes, or other important matters.

In such cases, the information will be processed only for the purposes that prevent its deletion. Once those reasons cease to exist, We will delete the relevant User information as soon as reasonably possible.

We may transfer Users' personal data across borders. Such transfers will be subject to applicable legal requirements and restrictions intended to provide an adequate level of protection for Users' personal data.

Data collected by Us and shared with external parties is collected automatically and does not, by itself, facilitate identification of the User.

Information about a User obtained from Third parties will be transferred in accordance with the applicable security requirements established by the personal data processing rules of each such Third party.

Information provided directly by Users will not be transferred across borders to Third parties, except where required or permitted by applicable law.

After receiving such a request, certain functionality of the Service may become unavailable, and information associated with the User may be deleted where applicable.
